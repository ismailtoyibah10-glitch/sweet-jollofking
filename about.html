About Jollof King

Founded by passionate food lovers, Jollof King is dedicated to celebrating West Africa's iconic rice dish. We believe every plate should have perfect spice balance, deep rich color, and that essential touch of smokiness.

Our goal is to share delicious recipes and help everyone enjoy the amazing taste of homemade Nigerian Jollof rice.
